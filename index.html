How it works

Five minutes. Then a toll.

One shared timer across all three sites, so switching from Shorts to Reels doesn't buy you a fresh start.

  1. Watch like normal

    Shorts, Reels and X all draw from the same 5-minute budget. Nothing changes until it runs out.

  2. Hit the lock

    The page is covered. Wait 30 seconds, then type this sentence by hand and press Enter: "I am choosing, of my own free will, to keep watching short videos"

  3. Pay, or walk away

    Type it right and you get 2 more minutes. Get it wrong and the wait doubles. Or just close the tab.

Step away for 10 minutes and the timer resets on its own.

Why friction

A speed bump, not a prison.

Blockers that hide Shorts already exist. They're great if you want short videos gone for good. This is for people who don't. You like a few clips. You just don't like looking up forty minutes later.

Short-form feeds are built so that continuing is the default and stopping takes effort. TypeToll flips that. Stopping is free. Continuing costs a wait and a sentence you have to mean enough to type.

You can always get past it, and you can always uninstall it. The point isn't to make watching impossible. It's to make every extra round a decision instead of a reflex.

Willpower default

Relies on you noticing, mid-scroll, that it's time to stop. The feed is designed so you don't.

Hard blockers all or nothing

Hide Shorts or Reels entirely. Effective if you want them gone. If you still enjoy some, all-or-nothing is the wrong tool.

TypeToll friction

Short videos stay available. Each extra few minutes asks for a pause and a deliberate yes.

Mashing the keyboard gets expensive.

Privacy

FAQ

Fair questions.

Can't I just uninstall it?

Yes, any time. TypeToll is a speed bump, not a prison. The goal is to stop the half-conscious "one more" scroll, not to fight you when you've genuinely decided to watch. If you find yourself uninstalling it, that was a decision too.

Does it work on mobile?

Chrome on desktop for now. Android is in the works.

What data do you collect?

None. Your timer state is stored locally in your browser, and the extension makes no network requests of its own. Your browsing history and page content never leave your device. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I change the sentence?

Not yet. The current version uses the default sentence. Writing your own (for example, "I said I'd finish my assignment tonight") is planned for Pro in a later version.

What happens if I'm offline?

Nothing changes. TypeToll runs entirely in your browser and never needs a connection.

Why can't I paste the sentence?

Because pasting takes no thought. Typing it out is the toll: it takes a few seconds of attention, which is usually enough to notice whether you actually want to keep going.

What counts toward the 5 minutes?

Time spent on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and X adds up on one shared timer. Regular YouTube videos aren't counted. If you're away from all three for 10 minutes, the timer resets.
