Privacy policy

Your browsing stays in your browser.

Effective date: September 25, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no analytics, no servers of ours.
  • Timer state is stored locally in your browser.
  • The extension makes no network requests. Your browsing history and page content never leave your device.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

Who we are

TypeToll is a Chrome extension made by an independent developer, mao-glitc. In this policy, "we" means the maker of TypeToll. Questions can be sent through GitHub Issues.

What is stored on your device

TypeToll stores the following using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage). It stays on your computer and is not sent to us.

DataWhy
Timer state: seconds watched, time remaining, lock status, timestamps of the last activity and of any current wait, and the number of misses in a rowTo run the shared 5-minute timer, the lock screen, the doubling penalty and the 10-minute reset
The effective settings: limit, unlock time, sentence and which sites are enabled (fixed defaults in the current version)So that the lock screen on every open tab uses the same values

TypeToll does not record which videos or posts you view, URLs you visit, or anything you type other than checking the unlock sentence on the spot. The typed sentence is compared locally and not saved.

The extension only runs on YouTube, Instagram and X, which it needs to measure time on Shorts, Reels and X and to show the lock screen there.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. TypeToll makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no license checks, no update pings to us. (Chrome itself updates extensions through the Chrome Web Store, as it does for every extension.)

Paid features

The current version is free and has nothing to buy. A paid Pro upgrade is planned. If it ships, and it requires any network request (for example, to check a license key with a payment provider), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and describe exactly what is sent.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No collection of browsing history, page content, or the videos and posts you view.
  • No use of your data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

Deleting your data

Everything the extension stores lives in your browser. Uninstalling TypeToll removes it.

Children

TypeToll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change ships.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub Issues. Please don't post personal information there; issues are public.
